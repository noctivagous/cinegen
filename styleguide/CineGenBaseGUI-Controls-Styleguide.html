Group boxes (fieldset)

Production classes in source/css/CineGenBaseGUI-controls-extra.css. Three variants loosely mapped to AppKit NSBox roles: secondary (flat bordered tile), titled (chrome strip + body), primary (recessed well for dense or sensitive controls). Hover preview popover

A singleton position: fixed div (#ov-col-preview) lives in the app HTML. On mouseenter of any .ov-col-item or .ov-row-item, showOvPreview(el, childIdx, itemIdx) populates it with item data and positions it just to the right of the row (flips left if near the viewport edge). A 120 ms debounce on mouseleave prevents flicker; entering the popover itself cancels the hide timer. Controlled by the "Preview on hover" toggle (_ovShowHoverPreview).
